# Practical playbook

## Outcome and scope

Explain the result the reader can work toward, the relevant context and the original video. State whether the video contains a method. Do not attribute a proposed procedure to an author who did not present it.

## Prepare

List necessary skills, tools, versions, data, access and constraints supported by available evidence. Mark unverified prerequisites.

## Method overview

Explain the author's actual method and its dependencies. Include the neutral Mermaid overview and links to method cards. Keep adaptations distinct from the source.

## Worked example

Define consistent inputs, identifiers and starting conditions. Mark synthetic data, assumptions and adaptation choices.

## Procedure

For each supported step, provide its purpose, dependencies, inputs, actions, decisions, expected output, checks and troubleshooting. Place transcript timestamps and useful screenshots beside the corresponding explanation.

Use headings for navigation, fenced code for commands, readable tables and relative image paths. Every screenshot needs a concise caption containing its timestamp and observation. Include neutral Mermaid step diagrams when they aid understanding.

## Verify the outcome

Distinguish what was observed in the video, executed locally, inferred or proposed. Document actual results and remaining prerequisites. A fully described example has not necessarily been run.

## Alternatives and limitations

Include alternatives supported by the supplied material, plus any justified adaptations. Keep unresolved questions visible. Do not silently fill essential gaps with unverified general knowledge.

## Supplementary findings

Include this section only if the user separately requested external sources. Preserve the source version and document what changed, why and with what evidence. Deepening alone does not authorize supplementary research.

## Evidence

Link to the video, timestamped passages, retained screenshots, method cards and evidence registry. Add supplementary sources only when explicitly requested and actually consulted. There is no source quota.
