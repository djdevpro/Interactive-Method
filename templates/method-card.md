# Method card

Complete this detailed card after the user requests deeper analysis. Start from the video, transcript and screenshots already inspected during the first overview. Mark absent or uncertain information. Consult supplementary sources only when the user separately requests them.

## Identity and provenance

Method ID, title, primary source and timestamped intervals, including separate passages. Link to the JSON card and supporting evidence.

## Method presented by the author

Intended outcome, conditions of use and assumptions. Describe the steps with their inputs, actions, decisions, outputs and success criteria as stated or shown.

## Open questions

Implicit prerequisites, missing transitions, ambiguous definitions, unverified behavior and dependencies on another method. Unanswered questions can remain open without triggering a search for supplementary sources.

## Optional research and enriched method

Include this section only if the user explicitly requested supplementary sources. For each researched question, record the sources consulted, the conclusion, objections, alternatives and conditions of use. Explain the effect on practice while preserving the author's version. A request for a complete analysis or playbook alone does not authorize supplementary research.

## Justified changes

| Step | Addition, clarification, correction or option | Rationale and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |

Distinguish changes supported by the supplied material from proposals adapted to the reader's context. Do not present a proposal as a verified fact.

## Example and checks

Inputs for the running example, concrete actions, expected output, checks and troubleshooting. Distinguish a proposed example from a result that was actually executed.

## Remaining limitations

Describe unknowns that affect whether the method can be followed or adapted, and what would resolve them. Do not require supplementary sources merely to complete this card.
